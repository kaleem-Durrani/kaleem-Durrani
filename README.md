# Kaleem Durrani

Founder of [Kairos Tech](https://kairos-pk.com). I build the systems businesses run on — point-of-sale, inventory, and accounting software in production at multiple retail businesses, for clients in Pakistan and the US.

## Selected work

**[Agora POS](https://kairos-pk.com/projects/Agora)** — enterprise point-of-sale with a full double-entry accounting engine underneath it: general ledger and sub-ledger architecture, multi-method inventory (per-item, by-weight, by-length, serialized), role-based access control, fiscal period management, and real-time financial reporting. Offline-first Electron desktop app with network sync, shipped on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

**Krysos** *(in development)* — ERP and point-of-sale for gold and silver traders and jewellery businesses. Bullion-aware accounting where a debt can be denominated in metal, not just currency: per-contact metal ledgers with booked rates, realized P&L measured at settlement, customer custody metal kept strictly outside trading balances, and a live headline of the shop's daily net metal exposure.

Most of my work lives in private repositories — client systems processing real transactions don't get open-sourced. The problems behind them are worth talking about, though. Ask me about:

- Implementing double-entry bookkeeping and a chart of accounts from scratch
- Offline-first architecture and sync for multi-location businesses

## In the open

**[Finite Automata Simulator](https://github.com/kaleem-Durrani/Finite-Automata-Simulator)** — a visual DFA editor and simulator built with Python and Pygame. Draw machines on an infinite canvas, then step through a string one symbol at a time — animated transitions, a moving read head, and rejections that explain *why*. Includes Moore minimisation, union / intersection / difference / complement constructions, and an equivalence check that returns the shortest distinguishing word when two machines disagree.
